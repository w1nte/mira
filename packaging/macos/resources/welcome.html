Mira indexes your photos, recognises the people in them, tags them and makes them searchable, all on this Mac.

It is installed into the Applications folder.

Already installed? Then this updates it: the app is replaced, your library (settings, database, thumbnails in ~/Library/Application Support/vision-tagger) is kept, and its database is migrated to the new version. A backup copy of the database is made first. A running Mira is closed first; a running pipeline is paused and continues on the next start.

Needs macOS 15 or newer on a Mac with Apple silicon.
